Blend: Valery

Roast: Medium

---

Roast: Medium
Taste: BOLD, full body, earthy, BITTER (good amount of bitterness)

---

Blend: The Sicilian

a bit less strong and intense than Rugan

---

Blend: Rugan
Composition: 50% Arabica, 50% Robusta (HIGHEST Robusta)
Roast: Medium plus
Taste: EXTREMELY BOLD, EXTREMELY BITTER, heavy body, maximum intensity
Flavors: Earthy, chocolate
Best for: People who want THE MOST BITTER, THE STRONGEST, most intense coffee

---

Blend: Max
Composition: 70% Arabica, 30% Robusta
Roast: Medium
Taste: Balanced with intensity, smooth but strong, GOOD BITTERNESS
Flavors: Dark chocolate, almonds
Best for: People who want strong and bitter but still balanced

# TASTE SCALE

ACIDIC SIDE (bright, lighter, more acidity):
- No.1 (90% Arabica, 10% Robusta) - Balanced with more acidic character
- Inigo (80% Arabica, 20% Robusta) - Balanced, light fruitiness  
- Buttercup (100% Arabica) - Delicate, gentle, sweet

BALANCED SIDE (middle, smooth):
- SIX (100% Arabica) - Full body, sweet, gentle (can be acidic for some)
- Valery (80% Arabica, 20% Robusta) - Chocolatey, very low acidity
- Andre (75% Arabica, 25% Robusta) - Bold but balanced, low acidity

BITTER SIDE (strong, intense, bold):
- Max (70% Arabica, 30% Robusta) - Strong with balance, low acidity
- The Sicilian (60% Arabica, 40% Robusta) - Very bitter, Italian style
- Rugan (50% Arabica, 50% Robusta) - Most bitter, strongest

QUICK MATCHING:
- Customer wants BITTER → Rugan, The Sicilian, Max
- Customer wants BALANCED → SIX, Valery, Andre
- Customer wants ACIDIC → No.1, Inigo, Buttercup

ACIDITY LEVELS (if customer mentions sour/acidic concerns):
- HIGH ACIDITY: No.1, SIX, Buttercup, Inigo
- LOW ACIDITY: Valery, Andre, The Sicilian, Rugan, Max
